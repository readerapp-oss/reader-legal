Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 26, 2026

Reader App ("Reader," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Reader mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Account information — your email address and display name, used for authentication and your profile.
  • Reading data — books you shelve, rate, review, and track, including reading progress and session times. This data powers your personal library and recommendations.
  • Social data — follows, likes, feed posts, replies, and list activity you choose to create.

We do not collect:

  • Location data
  • Contacts or address book information
  • Health or fitness data
  • Financial information (payments are handled entirely by Apple via the App Store)

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the app, including your shelves, reading progress, and social features.
  • Generate personalized book recommendations based on your reading history and ratings.
  • Display your public profile, reviews, and activity to other users (as you choose).
  • Send notifications you have opted into (e.g., new followers, replies to your posts).
  • Improve the app through aggregated, anonymized usage patterns.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure) with row-level security policies ensuring users can only access their own private data. Authentication is handled via Supabase Auth with industry-standard encryption.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (privacy policy)
  • Google Books API — book metadata and covers (privacy policy)
  • Open Library API — additional book metadata (privacy policy)
  • Apple App Store — subscription payments (privacy policy)
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (privacy policy)

We do not sell your personal information to any third party. We do not serve ads.

Subscriptions

Reader offers a free tier and an optional paid subscription (Reader+). Subscriptions are processed entirely through the Apple App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data at any time through the app.
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us at the email below.
  • Export your reading data by request.

Children's Privacy

Reader is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
