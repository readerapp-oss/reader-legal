Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Reader collects what it needs to run a reading tracker and a reading community. We don't sell your personal information, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Who we are

Reader is an independent app operated by Matthew Friedman ("Reader", "we"). Questions or requests: mattreaderteam@gmail.com.

How we use it

  • To run Reader: your account, shelves, feed, clubs, notifications and reading stats.
  • To show your public content to other readers, with spoilers gated by each reader's progress.
  • To keep Reader safe: content filtering, reports, blocking and enforcing our Terms.
  • To process subscriptions and restore purchases.
  • To fix bugs and improve the app, using diagnostics and aggregated usage.
  • To contact you about your account, security or changes to these policies.

What's public

Your display name, username, photo, bio, posts, replies, reviews, ratings, public lists and public club activity can be seen by other Reader users. Private club content is visible only to members. Your shelves and reading progress may appear to people you share them with, or in features like "Friends reading now". Settings control what you share.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety, or as part of a sale or reorganisation of Reader (in which case this policy continues to apply).

Book information comes from public catalogues (such as Open Library, Hardcover and Google Books) and isn't personal data. "Buy" links take you to stores such as Amazon or Bookshop.org; some may be affiliate links, but we don't send stores your identity.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account in Settings, we delete your profile, shelves, reading activity and uploaded files, and revoke Sign in with Apple. Posts and replies other people interacted with show as "[deleted]" so conversations still make sense. Backups are overwritten within 30 days. We keep limited records when the law requires it, or to handle abuse reports.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and content, or delete any post, at any time.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Turn notifications off in Settings or iOS Settings.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or a correction, by emailing us.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to some uses. We'll answer within 30 days. We don't sell or "share" personal information for cross-context advertising as California law defines it.

Children

Reader is for people 13 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we delete the account. Parents can contact us.

Security and where data is stored

Data is stored with our providers, mainly in the United States, and protected by access controls and encryption in transit. No system is perfectly secure; tell us if you find a problem.

Changes

We'll post changes here and update the date. For significant changes, we'll tell you in the app.
